About us

Care as it should be.

Hamilton George Care is one of the United Kingdom's most respected live-in care providers. Family founded and fully regulated in the UK, we are built on a simple belief: that older people deserve to stay in the homes they love, with carers they trust.

Every family we serve is different, so every arrangement we create is considered, personal and delivered to a standard we would want for our own. That quiet commitment to doing things properly has shaped everything we do, and it is the foundation we now hope to build upon in Florida.
